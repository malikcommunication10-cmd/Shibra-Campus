🧩 Other / custom entries

Any other key–value entries stored in the settings table (for example from the old settings page).

👩‍🏫 Payroll rules

Used in Staff & Payroll to calculate the leave deduction.

Deduction = base salary ÷ working days × (leaves − free leaves). Each month's payroll keeps the amount it was created with.

📚 Book store

Used in Book Store to flag books that need restocking.

A title is shown as “Low stock” when its stock is at or below this number.

📅 School year

Used by school-year-based records and reports.

🔌 Supabase connection

The connection is set once in config.js and shared by all pages.

Download a copy of all your data as one JSON file. Keep it somewhere safe.

Includes students, fees, accounts, books, staff, payroll, settings, follow-up calls and increments (whichever exist).
