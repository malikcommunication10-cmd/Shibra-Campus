Attendance & salary rules

            
Daily salary = monthly base salary ÷ 30, regardless of the calendar month's length.

            
Security deductions are withheld from net salary and tracked as a school payable; an opening balance can be entered once in Accounts and carries forward. Security is refunded against the outstanding balance when paying staff. Sundays are off. Each unexcused absence is deducted; an Admin may excuse it with a reason, waiving its deduction, but it still disqualifies the no-absence bonus. No absences earns a one-day salary bonus; late marks count as present for that bonus. Each group of 3 unexcused late marks deducts one day. Historical payroll is not recalculated.
